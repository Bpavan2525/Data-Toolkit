Spreadsheet Cleaner

Upload a messy CSV or Excel file of customers, orders or leads. Python (pandas) tidies the headers, trims spaces, removes blank and duplicate rows, formats phone numbers, lowercases emails, and turns text dates and amounts into real dates and numbers.

Files are processed in memory and not stored. Max 4 MB.
